WHAT IS
FRACTURED?

Fractured SMP is not a normal survival server.

It is an ongoing Minecraft story where every player has a role to play.

Characters have their own goals, relationships, secrets, and conflicts. Major events are planned, but players are given room to react, improvise, and shape their characters.

Every decision can change what happens next.

PROTOCOL

RULES

Treat everyone with respect. Fractured is a collaborative story and every player deserves a safe and enjoyable environment.

All SMP-related communication and roleplay must be conducted in English.

Do not intentionally ruin planned events or deliberately sabotage story moments.

Your character only knows what they should know. Information discovered outside the roleplay should not automatically become character knowledge.

Do not control another player's character or decide their reactions and actions for them.

PvP, destruction, stealing, and other major actions should fit the story and the roleplay.

Do not leak scripts, unreleased lore, planned scenes, private information, or future story events.

This is a collaborative story. Create memorable moments, respect the people around you, and enjoy the experience.
